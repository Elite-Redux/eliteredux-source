# Actual Wiki Content

This is the content which will be shown in-game.

## Basic Information

### 	Talk to Nurse Joy

* Talk to Nurse Joy in any Pokémon Center to
* receive a DexNav+, Infinite Repel, Battle
* Items, all Pokéballs, all Berries, all
* Type Gems, and more.
*
* Each time you speak to her, all items
* are refilled.
*
* Mega Stones are the only items not given
* by Nurse Joy.

### Online Pokédex

* [https://dex.elite-redux.com](https://dex.elite-redux.com)
*
* The Online Dex shows all info on Pokémon,
* Abilities, Moves, Locations, & Trainers.
*
* You can also build teams there for
* theorycrafting.

### Pokémon Changes

* Almost every Pokémon has been improved for
* better balance and utility.
*
* Notable reworks:
* **Kecleon**: Colour Change activates before an
* attack hits. It changes Kecleon's type to
* the most favourable matchup. This makes it
* immune to attacks with a type-based
* immunity & resistant to all other attacks.

*
* **Eeveelutions**: All can devolve to Eevee,
* preserving movepools. All eight share
* movepools, gaining unparalleled type
* coverage and varied STAB moves.
*
* **Hatterene**: Innate Pixilate, Magic Bounce,
* & Hyper Aggressive (Parental Bond clone).
* Ability Twisted Dimensions sets Trick Room
* for 3 turns on entry.

*
* **Dewgong**: Abilities: Fur Coat, Ice Scales,
* Arctic Fur (lesser version of both).
* Innate North Wind auto-sets Aurora Veil
* for 3 turns on entry.
*
* **Gliscor**: Has Poison Heal. Gets Rough Skin
* for better physical defense. Tectonize
* ability turns Facade into Ground-type.

*
* **Pyukumuku**: 600 BST, innate Unaware.
* Pressure (buffed: casts Haze on entry
* and its vanilla function).
*
* **Braviary**: Stacks Defiant, Flock (Flying
* Overgrow), buffed Big Pecks. Can have
* Sheer Force or Gale Wings for immense power.

*
* **Phione**: Innate High Tide: uses 50BP Surf
* after any Water move. Field Explorer
* empowers 'field' moves (Headbutt, Surf).
* Soul-Heart ability option.
*
* Check the Online Pokédex for details.

### Move Changes

* Many moves have improved accuracy & power.
*
* OHKO moves (e.g. Fissure) now deal ~120
* damage with 80% accuracy.
*
* Some moves have new effects; others are
* completely reworked. Most moves have a
* usable niche.
*
* Further details are in the Online Pokédex.

### Quality of Life Improvements

* **Portable PC**: Access from Start Menu
* (disabled for Elite Four).
*
* No need to teach HMs. Fly to any visited
* location with any Pokémon. Other HMs
* unlock as you complete Gyms.
*
* Register multiple items for easy access
* using L button.

### Battle Changes

* All Pokémon now have 31 IVs by default.
* Use Iron Pill to set Speed to 0 if needed
* (and back to 31).
*
* Ace/Elite Modes include Sleep Clause (only
* one enemy Pokémon can be put to sleep).
*
* All moves have PP Max. Held items regen
* after battles. Pokémon heal after most
* battles. Some are gauntlets (no healing).

### Battle UI Changes

* Press L during battles for this wiki.
* Shortcut changeable in Settings > Custom (R).
*
* See a move's effectiveness before use.
* This considers abilities/innates like
* Levitate or Sap Sipper!
*
* STAB (50% Same Type Attack Bonus) is
* shown with a + icon.
*
* Trainer battles can be forfeited (counts
* as a loss), returning to last Poké Center.

### Acquiring Pokémon

* Get one of each Pokéball from Nurse Joy,
* all with 100% catch rate. Pokéballs are
* not consumed on use.
*
* Upgraded DexNav+ auto-searches Pokémon
* in an area. Spend BP to get Pokémon
* directly without battling.

*
* Shiny rates boostable to 1/5 in Options.
* All Gen 1-9 Pokémon are available.
*
* Buy Redux Pokémon for BP in Adoption
* Center (more unlock with progress).

### Switching Nature

* Switch a Pokémon’s nature directly in its
* Summary Screen.
*
* Natures increase one base stat by 10%
* and decrease another by 10%.
*
* Indicated by up/down arrows on summary
* screen next to affected stats.

### Changing Hidden/Secret Power

* Use Type Gems (from Nurse Joy) to change
* a Pokémon’s Hidden Power type and
* Secret Power (physical version) type.

### Setting EVs

* Directly assign EVs in Summary Screen;
* no grinding needed.
*
* Each stat: up to 252 EVs. Total: 510 EVs.
*
* Typically, distribute 252 EVs into two
* best stats for optimal results.
*
* HP is often more useful than one defense,
* unless base HP is very high and defenses
* are much lower.

### Learning Moves

* Learn new moves easily in Party or Move
* menu (press R).
*
* Certain moves still require leveling up.
* These are in the Level list. Other lists
* show moves by category.
*
* Unlock new moves after each gym in Elite
* caps, until ~2/3s into game. Some learn
* moves faster/slower.

### Evolutions

* Unusual evolutions are now level-based.
* Summary screen shows if a Pokémon can
* evolve and at what level.
*
* Potential Mega Evolutions also shown on
* Summary screen (even without the stone),
* but unusable without it.

*
* Evolve from Party menu; choose evolution
* for branched paths.
*
* Level up from Party menu. Level Caps &
* scaling enemies prevent overleveling.

## Ability Information

### Innates

* Pokémon have 1-3 abilities, changeable
* in summary menu. Most have 3 choices.
* View ability details by selecting them.
*
* Pokémon also have 3 'innates' tied to
* their species, always active with ability.
*
* On higher difficulties, most innates are
* locked. Defeat Gym Leaders and raise
* level cap to unlock them.

### New Abilities

* Elite Redux has 500+ new abilities.
*
* Some are for specific Pokémon to make
* them stronger/more interesting. Some are
* combo abilities, some new generic ones.
*
* Examples: Chloroplast (moves act as if
* in Sun); Phantom Pain (Ghost moves hit
* Normal); Fungal Infection (contact moves
* inflict Leech Seed); Fort Knox (blocks
* enemy offensive power-up abilities).

### Changed Abilities

* Many existing abilities revised to be
* more powerful and have a niche.
*
* Stall: 30% less damage if not moved.
* Battle/Shell Armour: 20% less damage,
* block crits.
* Big Pecks: Now a Tough Claws clone.
*
* Storm Drain/Lightning Rod: Boost higher
* offensive stat on activation.
* Frisk: Disables enemy item for 2 turns.

## Battle Information

### General Information about Status Effects

* Elite Redux changes some effects like
* Confusion & Infatuation, and adds new
* ones: Bleed, Fear, Frostbite (replaces
* Freeze).

### Burn, Paralysis, Poison, Sleep

* These statuses are unchanged.
*
* Burn: 1/8 HP damage/turn, halves phys. atk.
*
* Paralysis: Reduces Speed, 25% chance to
* be unable to move.
*
* Poison: 1/8 HP damage/turn. Badly Poisoned
* starts 1/16, increases by 1/16 each turn.
*
* Sleep: Unable to move.

### New Status Effects

* **Bleed**
*   A unique, non-volatile status effect.
*   Mainly from Keen Edge boosted moves, but
*   not exclusive to them.
*
*   Deals 1/16 max HP damage per turn.
*   Prevents healing effects and negates
*   stat boosts. Rock & Ghost-types immune.
*
*   Healing moves cure Bleed but won't heal
*   HP that turn.

*
* **Fear**
*   Traps the target for 2 turns.
*   Inflicted Pokémon take 50% more damage.
*
*   Moves: Scary Face, Terror Charge,
*   Worry Seed.
*
* **Frostbite** (replaces Freeze)
*   Deals 1/16 HP damage per turn to non-Ice
*   types. Halves user's special atk damage.

### Changed Status Effects

* **Confusion**
*   No RNG. Confused Pokémon takes 33% of
*   damage it deals as recoil.
*
* **Infatuation**
*   Reworked: Infatuated targets deal 50%
*   less damage (physical & special).

### General Weather Information

* Weather from moves/abilities: 8 turns
* (12 with Weather Rocks).
*
* **Hail/Snow**
*   Boosts Ice-types’ Defense by 50%.
*   Damages non-Ice types (1/16 HP/turn).
*   Blizzard ignores accuracy checks.
*
* **Sandstorm**
*   Damages non-Rock/Ground/Steel types
*   (1/16 HP/turn).
*   Boosts Rock-types’ Sp. Def by 50%.

*
* **Rain**
*   Boosts Water-type moves by 50%.
*   Weakens Fire-type moves by 50%.
*   Thunder & Hurricane ignore accuracy.
*
* **Sunlight**
*   Boosts Fire-type moves by 50%.
*   Weakens Water-type moves by 50%.
*   Prevents freezing.
*   Solar Beam/Blade become 1-turn moves.
*   Growth: Atk & Sp. Atk +2 stages.

*
* **Harsh Sunlight**
*   Boosts Fire moves, nullifies Water moves.
*
* **Heavy Rain**
*   Boosts Water moves, nullifies Fire moves.
*
* **Strong Winds**
*   Reduces super-effective damage vs Flying
*   types to neutral.

### New Weather: Fog

* Elite Redux reintroduces Fog as unique
* weather: Eerie Fog. Effects:
*
* - Drains non-Ghost/Psychic +stats by 1
*   stage each turn.
* - Halves weather-based recovery (Synthesis).
* - 20% damage reduction for Ghost/Psychic.

*
* - Revival Blessing fails.
* - Harvest fails.
* - All Curse instances become Ghost-type.
*
* - Weather Ball becomes Ghost, 2x power.
* - Castform (Foggy) form.
* - Some moves like Ominous Wind 2x power.

*
* Smoke Ball (held item) increases fog
* duration from 8 to 12 turns.

## Miscellaneous Information

### Permanent Mega Mode

* Unlocked after talking to Norman.
*
* Change Pokémon to Mega forms o/s battle
* via party menu (like normal form change).
* Still requires correct Mega Stone in bag.

*
* Pokémon stays Mega after battles.
* Allows multiple Megas on a team at once.
* Megas can hold other items!
*
* Normal in-battle Mega Evolution is still
* an option.
